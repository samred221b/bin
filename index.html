A Little Bit About Me

More Than a Designer

I'm an Addis Ababa-based graphic designer who thrives on transforming complex ideas into crystal-clear visual systems. My work is informed by 3+ years of agency and freelance experience, a deep understanding of layout design, and a dedication to cultural relevance. I don't just make things look good; I build visual assets that support a cohesive brand narrative and achieve tangible results.

My Expertise

Focusing on intentionality, I combine structured design principles with creative problem-solving to deliver projects that range from extensive brand guideline documents to high-impact event visual systems.

Brand Development

Creating comprehensive brand guidelines, identity systems, and visual language assets for enduring brand equity.

Layout & Editorial

Designing grid-based reports, publications, magazines, and other editorial materials for maximum readability and structure.

Event & Campaign

Developing cohesive visual identities across all physical and digital touchpoints for major events and social campaigns (OOH/digital).
